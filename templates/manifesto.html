Why Did I Made This?

I feel like social media isn't really social anymore. At some point, it stopped being a place where you went to see what your friends were doing and turned into a giant machine for consuming mass-produced content, brainrot, and, increasingly, AI slop. Social media platforms are owned by a handful of asshole billionares and their entire business model is basically getting you to stay on their app for as long as possible. Endless scrolling, notifications, algorithmic recommendations. Everything is designed to keep you in a dopamine loop so you don't close the app (and even when you close it you will open it again 5 minutes later). The more time you spend there, the more ads they can show you and the more information they can learn about you to later sell it to anyone. And honestly, even before all of that, things like likes and reactions already made social media kind of shitty. Instead of just posting something because you wanted your friends to know what you've been up to, it became a competition. Take the right picture. Say the right thing. Get enough likes. Show how good your life is, how hard you are working, or how early you woke up, and then, stand by for validation... We're all pretty aware by now that spending hours staring at algorithmically optimized feeds probably isn't doing our brains any favors. It has been shown again and again how bad this actually is for you. And with the assholes running the show missinformation and far right leaning propaganda are just making things worse. So I made this. It's just a small place for us to share what we're up to with people who actually care. You don't need to post every day. You don't have to choreograph and overthink what you post. You don't need to get likes (you actually can't get likes, I will ot be adding that feature). You don't even need to add any friends. Maybe you can just post something once a month (or more often if somehting cool happens) what you've been doing, a picture from a trip, something that you are excited about, whatever. Then you can check in and see what your friends have been up to too. No algorithm. No endless feed. No likes. No reactions. No ads. Just a little corner of the internet for keeping up with your friends. P.S. I made this with the idea of keepig the home feed just for life updates, random thoughts, and day to day interactions. However, because some of my friends love ranting and are really good at it, I added another feed for when you want to share something that is on your mind that has nothing to do with you, maybe you want to complain about MONSANTO, or maybe you want to share a delicious pozole recipe. Added to the Opinion Pieces (name in progress) feed. P.P.S. With the same idea in mind, I added a Creative Writing feed. A lot of you are talented storytellers and might want to share something cool that you created, like a story, a song, a fucking poem. Anything that you wrote and want to share, chuck it in the Creative Writing feed.
